The 2021 and 2023 pages expose public-use DOS/Windows, gzip, CSV, replicate-weight files and technical documentation. 2023 main CSV is approximately 145 MB according to Census.

Conceptual content includes interactions with family/friends/neighbors, group membership/participation, formal volunteering, informal helping, political/civic engagement, and donations.
