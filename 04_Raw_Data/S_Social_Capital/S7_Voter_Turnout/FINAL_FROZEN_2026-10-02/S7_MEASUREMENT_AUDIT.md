# S7 Measurement Audit
- Construct (project decision P06 v1.1/v1.2, 2026-09-24): turnout is an election-wave variable; observed only in general-election years; never forward-filled. This package keeps that rule: odd-year rows are model estimates of a latent participation propensity, not turnout.
- Direct source: UF Election Lab (McDonald) 1980-2022 General Election Turnout Rates v1.2. VEP = VAP*(1-NONCITIZEN_PCT) - INELIGIBLE_FELONS_TOTAL; VEP turnout = TOTAL_BALLOTS_COUNTED / VEP.
- Coverage: 12 general elections (2000-2022, even years) x 50 states = 600 direct cells; DC excluded. 2020 Montana and Pennsylvania appear in the source as "Montana*"/"Pennsylvania*" and are used (asterisk stripped).
- Numerator: TOTAL_BALLOTS_COUNTED in 560 cells; in 40 cells (2000: 12, 2002: 15, 2004: 4, 2006: 6, 2008: 3) ballots are not reported and VOTE_FOR_HIGHEST_OFFICE is substituted, as recommended in the source documentation. Ballots/highest-office ratio where both exist: 1.010-1.023 by year. A model offset for substituted cells (M2b) is ~0 and not retained; cells carry validation_risk = 1.
- Presidential vs midterm: same numerator/denominator definition; numerator office differs by cycle (president vs. Senate/governor). Handled by election-type measurement parameters (midterm alpha = -0.115, lambda = 0.901, tau = 0.043; presidential anchor alpha = 0, lambda = 1, tau = 0.010). Observed values never rescaled; raw values in observed_turnout_vep and S7_DIRECT_OBSERVATIONS_FINAL.csv.
- Reproduction: computed TOTAL_BALLOTS_COUNTED/VEP equals published VEP_TURNOUT_RATE in 560/560 cells (max |diff| 5.0e-5, rounding). Project P06 presidential file: 281/300 identical, 19 missing there (ballots not reported).
