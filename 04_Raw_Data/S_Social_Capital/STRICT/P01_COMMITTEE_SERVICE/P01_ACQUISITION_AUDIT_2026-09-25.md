# P01 Committee Service — acquisition audit (2026-09-25)

Putnam original construct: served on committee for local organization (percent).

## Modern federal source checked
U.S. Current Population Survey Civic Engagement Supplement, harmonized variable CECOMSERV.
Question/construct: whether respondent served on a committee OR as an officer of any group or organization in the past 12 months.
Observed waves: November 2008, 2009, 2010, 2011, 2013.
State geography is available in CPS.

## Equivalence decision
The modern CPS item combines two Putnam indicators: committee service (P01) and officer service (P02). Therefore it cannot be used as an exact P01 measure without mixing constructs. Under the project strict-equivalence rule, no numeric P01 series is populated from CECOMSERV.

Status: BLOCKED_EQUIVALENCE_FOR_STRICT_P01.
Candidate secondary/bridge variable: CECOMSERV combined organizational leadership.
No interpolation. No substitution.

Evidence:
- IPUMS CPS CECOMSERV description/availability.
- U.S. Census CPS Civic Engagement supplement documentation for 2008-2013.
