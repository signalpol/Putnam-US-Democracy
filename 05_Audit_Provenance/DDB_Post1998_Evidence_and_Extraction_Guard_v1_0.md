# DDB Post-1998 Evidence and Extraction Guard v1.0
Date: 2026-09-24

## Verified continuation evidence
Published studies establish that DDB Life Style data continued into the 2000s.
- Herbst analyses use DDB Life Style Survey observations through 2005 and acknowledge direct assistance from DDB Worldwide in obtaining the survey.
- Published Fischer figures explicitly use DDB variables ENTHOME and VISFRD into the 2000s.
- Other published civic-health work reports the DDB "Most people are honest" series through 2005/2006.
These are evidence that later waves/items existed; they are NOT permission to reconstruct microdata from figures.

## Geography evidence
Published research has aggregated DDB "Most people are honest" responses within state-year and county units, demonstrating that geographic aggregation was technically possible in versions available to those researchers.
This does not establish that every post-1998 public/restricted file contains the same geography or that the data are publicly redistributable.

## Prohibited shortcut
One published state-year implementation replaced low-count states with adjacent-state averages and then applied three-year moving averages.
The Putnam U.S. Democracy Project will NOT adopt these replacements in the canonical strict series:
- no adjacent-state substitution;
- no moving-average filling of canonical observations;
- no reconstruction from published charts;
- no treating smoothed published estimates as raw DDB observations.

## Required post-1998 acquisition object
Target: original or author-deposited DDB Life Style microdata for 1999-2005/2006 with:
YEAR, STATE/geography, WEIGHT, VISFRD, ENTHOME, HONEST and their labels/codes.

Priority routes:
1. replication materials from scholars who used 1999-2005/06 waves;
2. university/institutional archives;
3. direct scholarly request to the relevant author/data rightsholder;
4. rightsholder-provided exact state-year tabulations if microdata cannot be released.

## Acceptance gate
Before joining to Putnam public 1975-1998 DDB:
- wording equivalence;
- response-code equivalence;
- weight continuity;
- sampling-frame/design continuity;
- state coding continuity;
- item availability by year;
- valid N by state-year;
- raw provenance and SHA-256.

If any gate fails, retain later data as a separate S2/S3 series rather than S1 strict continuation.
