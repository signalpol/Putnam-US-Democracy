# Ferragina E Master v2 COMPLETE — Gini completion provenance

Date: 2026-09-25

- 2000–2005: U.S. Census Bureau, Hisnanick & Rogers, *Household Income Inequality Measures Based on the ACS Data: 2000–2005*. Exact state-level reported Gini point estimates from Tables 1–6. No interpolation.
- 2006–2019 and 2021–2023: existing ACS 1-year B19083 series retained unchanged.
- 2020: the Census Bureau did not release the standard 2020 ACS 1-year data product because of COVID-19. To preserve a complete annual 50-state analytical panel on the same ACS1 scale, 2020 is explicitly harmonized as the arithmetic midpoint of each state's 2019 and 2021 ACS1 Gini. It is NOT labeled observed. Robustness analyses must (a) exclude 2020 and (b) optionally substitute the observed 2020 ACS 5-year B19083 value.
- 50 states only; DC excluded.
- Complete panel: 1,200 state-years; Gini missing = 0.
- Measurement-regime caveat: 2000–2004 were ACS testing-program years; 2005 full implementation.
