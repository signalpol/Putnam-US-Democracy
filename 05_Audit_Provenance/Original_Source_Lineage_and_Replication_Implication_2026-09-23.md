# Original Source Lineage and Replication Implication — 2026-09-23

## Verified source lineage
Putnam's state Social Capital Index combines 14 indicators from multiple source families and periods rather than a single annual 50-state panel.

### Roper Social and Political Trends
Public cumulative archive: 1973-1994 (Putnam site describes selected questions through the 1990s).
Core exact battery includes:
- officer of club/organization
- committee of local organization
- public meeting on town or school affairs
Post-1994 data are described by the Roper Center as proprietary through RoperASW/GfK Consumer Life.

Implication: CPS analogues are not strict continuations of the Roper battery unless wording/statistic equivalence is demonstrated.

### DDB Needham Life Style
Putnam public archive: 1975-1998.
Core exact measures include frequency/count or agreement items used for:
- club meetings
- volunteer work
- entertaining at home
- community project
- visiting friends
- perceived honesty

Implication: public Putnam/DDB series does not itself provide a 2000-2023 continuation. Modern substitutes must not be silently inserted into the canonical replication.

### GSS
Continues beyond 2000 and contains generalized trust and organizational-membership material.
State geography is not generally available in unrestricted public-use form at the level required for a reproducible 50-state panel.
Implication: exact construct may continue nationally, but state-level canonical use requires legitimate geographic access.

### Administrative indicators
Presidential turnout, civic/social organization density, and nonprofit density have plausible modern administrative continuations and should be matched to Putnam's original numerator/denominator definitions before canonical use.

## Critical methodological implication
A strict Putnam replication does NOT require pretending that all 14 indicators exist annually for 50 states. Putnam's original state index itself combined indicators measured over different periods/sources.

Therefore maintain two distinct empirical products:

A. STRICT PUTNAM REPLICATION/EXTENSION
- only exact or demonstrably compatible original measures
- preserve irregular waves and missingness
- construct comparable period/state indices only where measurement coverage supports them
- never fill gaps with non-equivalent proxies

B. MODERN LONGITUDINAL EXTENSION
- separately labeled modern measures/proxies
- used for annual 2000-2023 state-year analysis and robustness
- never represented as identical to Putnam's original 14-variable operationalization

This separation protects the primary scholarly contribution: test long-run U.S. democracy/social-capital/economic trajectories while making transparent which results are strict Putnam replication and which are modern measurement extensions.
