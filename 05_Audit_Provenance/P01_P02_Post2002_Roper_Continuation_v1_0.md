# P01/P02 Post-2002 Roper Continuation v1.0
Date: 2026-09-24

## New finding
P01 Committee Service and P02 Officer Service did not disappear after the 1973-2002 cumulative archive.

Published research using a nationwide RoperASW survey at the end of 2003 reports an Influentials battery asking whether respondents had, within the last year:
- served on a committee for some local organization;
- served as an officer for some club or organization;
alongside public-meeting and other civic/political participation items.

A separate published description of the Roper ASW Influential American battery gives the same committee/officer items.

## Implication
P01/P02 have:
1. exact original Roper SPT items;
2. official updated cumulative Roper file through 2002 at openICPSR;
3. documented same/near-identical RoperASW continuation in 2003.

Therefore 2002 is an archive boundary, not a demonstrated end of measurement.
