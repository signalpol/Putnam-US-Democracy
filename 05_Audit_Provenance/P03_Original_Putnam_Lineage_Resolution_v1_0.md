# P03 Original Putnam Lineage Resolution v1.0
Date: 2026-09-23

## Original Putnam operationalization — resolved
Bowling Alone endnote for the state social-capital index states that the civic-association measure is:
- the mean number of "civic and social associations";
- SIC 8640 as reported in Putnam's text/endnote;
- reported annually by the U.S. Commerce Department;
- years 1977-1992;
- divided by each state's population in each year.

A later methodological reproduction reports the source as USDOC 1977-1992 for all 50 states and the measure as civic/social organizations per 1,000 population.

Historical SIC documentation identifies the detailed class 8641 as Civic, Social, and Fraternal Associations. Putnam's 8640 notation is therefore treated as the historical industry-group/family notation requiring careful concordance documentation, not as evidence for a different construct.

## Modern classification
Census NAICS 813410 = Civic and Social Organizations.
Official definition: establishments primarily engaged in promoting the civic and social interests of their members.
