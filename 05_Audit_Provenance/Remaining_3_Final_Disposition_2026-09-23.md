# Remaining Three Variables — Final Disposition (2026-09-23)

Canonical panel: 50 U.S. states x 2000-2023. No interpolation or fabricated small-area estimates.

## P12 Entertain at Home
Original Putnam measurement is anchored in the DDB Needham Life Style archive. The historical archive ends before the canonical 2000-2023 panel. No verified modern annual or repeated 50-state source with measurement equivalence has been secured.

Disposition: STRUCTURAL MISSING for canonical 2000-2023 panel. Preserve DDB as historical construct anchor only.

## P13 Generalized Trust
The GSS contains the conceptually exact generalized-trust question, but public-use GSS does not provide unrestricted state identifiers sufficient for a reproducible 50-state state-year panel; detailed geography requires restricted access. The 2000 Social Capital Community Benchmark Survey is a strong Putnam-era measurement bridge but its community design covers only a subset of states and is not a 50-state state-representative panel.

Disposition: STRUCTURAL MISSING in the canonical public-data panel unless restricted GSS geography or a demonstrably equivalent 50-state source is obtained. GSS/SCCBS retained for construct validation only.

## P14 Perceived Honesty
Putnam's original honesty indicator is historically anchored in DDB/Market Facts and is distinct from generalized trust. No verified modern 2000-2023 50-state measurement-equivalent series has been secured.

Disposition: STRUCTURAL MISSING for canonical panel. Do not substitute P13 generalized trust.

## Research consequence
The empirical project must not require all 14 original indicators to be observed in every state-year. The canonical modern replication should:
1. preserve P01-P14 identities and missingness;
2. estimate the modern social-capital construct from empirically available indicators;
3. report indicator coverage by year/state;
4. use historical P12/P13/P14 sources for construct/measurement validation where appropriate;
5. run sensitivity specifications excluding structurally unavailable indicators rather than imputing them.

This disposition closes source hunting for P12/P13/P14 unless new restricted or measurement-equivalent data are explicitly obtained.
