# Remaining Putnam Variables — Source Architecture

Canonical panel: 50 U.S. states, 2000–2023. No interpolation in canonical analysis.

| Original Putnam indicator | Modern source candidate | Observed waves / coverage | Classification |
|---|---|---|---|
| Served on committee of local organization | CPS Civic Engagement CECOMSERV / PEQ6 | 2008, 2009, 2010, 2011, 2013 | COMBINED PROXY ONLY — officer + committee cannot be separated |
| Served as officer of club/organization | CPS Civic Engagement CECOMSERV / PEQ6 | 2008, 2009, 2010, 2011, 2013 | COMBINED PROXY ONLY — do not duplicate into two variables |
| Civic and social organizations per 1,000 population | Census CBP NAICS 813410 establishments / Census population | annual 2000–2023 target | ADMINISTRATIVE REPLICATION |
| Mean number of club meetings attended | CPS CEATTMEET can measure any group/organization meeting attendance | selected Civic Engagement waves | CONCEPTUAL PROXY; frequency equivalence requires audit |
| Spend a lot of time visiting friends | CPS CESOCCONTCT; supplemental CESOCIALIZE for neighbors | CESOCCONTCT: 2011, 2013, 2017, 2019, 2021, 2023 | MODERN SOCIABILITY PROXY |
| Mean number of times entertained at home | DDB Needham Life Style Archive | original historical series | ORIGINAL-SOURCE DEPENDENT; modern exact replacement not yet identified |
| Most people can be trusted | General Social Survey TRUST family | repeated GSS waves | ORIGINAL QUESTION FAMILY; state-year sample adequacy must be audited |
| Most people are honest | DDB Needham Life Style Archive | original historical series | ORIGINAL-SOURCE DEPENDENT; do not substitute neighborhood trust |

## Measurement guardrails

1. Never copy CECOMSERV into both committee and officer slots as if they were separately observed.
2. Preserve actual survey-wave years only; nonwave years remain missing.
3. Label modern replacements as proxies/replications rather than original Putnam measures.
4. Do not interpolate canonical values.
5. Keep source wording, universe, weights, and measurement-regime changes in provenance.
