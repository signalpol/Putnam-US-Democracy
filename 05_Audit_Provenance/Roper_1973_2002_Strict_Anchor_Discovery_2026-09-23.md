# Roper 1973-2002 Strict Anchor Discovery — 2026-09-23

A major source extension was identified in the openICPSR replication archive for Verba, Schlozman, and Brady, The Unheavenly Chorus (2012).

The archive explicitly states that it contains:
- Roper Social and Political Trends Data 1973-1994; and
- an updated Roper cumulative file covering 1973-2002.
File: Roper.sav (117.4 MB).

This is directly relevant to strict Putnam replication because the Roper SPT battery is one of Putnam's primary source families and contains the exact participation items including:
- served as officer of club/organization (P02);
- served on committee of local organization (P01);
- attended public meeting on town or school affairs (P07).

Research implication:
1. Do not treat P01/P02/P07 as necessarily unavailable after 1994.
2. Acquire and inspect Roper.sav variable labels, survey dates, state geography, weights, and exact question continuity for 1995-2002.
3. If exact items, state identifiers, and usable weights persist into 2000-2002, those observations become strict canonical anchors at the start of the project's 2000-2023 period.
4. Do not infer coverage beyond 2002 from this archive.
5. Modern CPS analogues remain robustness-only unless exact equivalence is independently established.
