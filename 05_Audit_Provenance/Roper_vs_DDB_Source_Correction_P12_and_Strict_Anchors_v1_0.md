# Roper vs DDB Source Correction — P12 and Strict Anchors v1.0
Date: 2026-09-23

## Correction
Do not infer that a similar Roper behavior item is the canonical Putnam source for P12.

Putnam's own research documentation identifies:
- Roper SPT: officer service, committee service, town/school public meeting and other participation/social-activity items.
- DDB Needham Life Style archive (1975-1999 listed on Putnam research page): annual-frequency leisure/social items including "Entertain at home" and "Work on a community project."

Bowling Alone's state-index source table / later reproductions identify the canonical P12 "mean number of times entertained at home in last year" as DDB Needham Life Style, not Roper.

Therefore:
- Roper "had friends in for evening" is conceptually related but NOT the canonical P12 continuation.
- It must not be promoted to strict P12 merely because wording/behavior is similar.
