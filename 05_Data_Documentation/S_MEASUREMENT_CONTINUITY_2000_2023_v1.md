## Non-negotiable continuity rule
These families overlap in concepts but are not automatically one metric. Never concatenate, average, rescale, interpolate, or backcast them into a synthetic annual 2000–2023 S series without a separately approved measurement-equivalence procedure.

## Gaps
No invented S observations for uncovered years. Missing remains missing.

## Before statistical analysis
Create variable-level crosswalks: exact question wording, response categories, universe, weighting variable, geography availability, and concept mapping to Putnam dimensions. Only demonstrably comparable items may be used in repeated-indicator robustness analysis.
