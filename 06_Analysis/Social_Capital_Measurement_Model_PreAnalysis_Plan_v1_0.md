# Social Capital Measurement Model — Pre-Analysis Plan v1.0
Date: 2026-09-23

## Principle
Do not impose a single Social Capital Index before testing dimensionality.

## Provisional theoretical blocks
A. Associational-Civic:
P01, P02, P03, P04, P05, P08, P09, P10

B. Civic-Political:
P06, P07

C. Informal Sociability:
P11, P12

D. Trust-Normative:
P13, P14

These are theoretical starting classifications, not empirical conclusions.

## Analysis sequence
1. Plot every indicator separately by year and coverage.
2. Test within-block covariance/correlation where overlapping observations exist.
3. Examine whether measurement relationships are stable over time.
4. Estimate block-specific standardized indices only when coverage and coherence permit.
5. Where sample/coverage permits, compare:
   - correlated-factor model;
   - higher-order social-capital factor;
   - simple standardized composite.
6. Compare block trajectories before constructing any global composite.
7. A global Social Capital index is allowed only if empirical covariance and measurement stability justify it.
8. If dimensions diverge, report them separately as the substantive result rather than forcing aggregation.

## Putnam replication rule
Strict Putnam observations retain original operational definitions and irregular timing. They serve as anchors for construct validity.

## Modern extension rule
Modern measures may expand annual coverage but must be labeled separately. Measurement invariance with strict anchors must be evaluated rather than assumed.

## Specific sensitivity tests
- Exclude Civic-Political P06/P07 from the social-capital composite to test possible conceptual overlap with democracy.
- Separate administrative density indicators P03/P08 from individual behavioral indicators.
- Separate formal associations from informal sociability.
- Estimate Trust-Normative independently.
- Compare results with and without indicators whose social meaning plausibly changed during the digital-participation era.

## Anti-circularity rule
No democracy outcome or component of the democracy DV may enter the social-capital measurement model. Political participation indicators P06/P07 require explicit sensitivity analysis because conceptual proximity to democratic participation can mechanically strengthen S-D associations.

## Missing-data rule
No interpolation to manufacture strict Putnam observations. Changing indicator availability cannot silently change index composition. Every reported composite must disclose its indicator basket and coverage.
