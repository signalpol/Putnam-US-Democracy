# Critical S_t literature/data-source discovery — 2026-09-25

## Hawes, Rocha & Meier (2013)
Social Capital in the 50 States: Measuring State-Level Social Capital, 1986–2004, State Politics & Policy Quarterly 13(1):121–138, DOI 10.1177/1532440012460057.

This is a direct Putnam-follow-on study. It explicitly constructs a dynamic state-level social-capital measure intended to parallel Putnam's operationalization. Source is MediaMark Research (MRI) Survey of the American Consumer, >20,000 individuals, biannual, contiguous 48 states. Published dataset coverage is 1986–2004, therefore overlaps our target for 2000–2004.

Key methodological facts:
- Putnam used 14 state-level items; Hawes et al. explicitly target the nonattitudinal Putnam components.
- Their measure covers organizational life, public affairs, community volunteerism; not informal sociability/social trust in the published index.
- All items available 1995–2004; earlier periods use available-item factor scores.
- Their averaged state measure correlates about .824 with Putnam's static index.
- Published paper states annual state trust estimates were infeasible with GSS and notes GSS PSU/state-representativeness limitation.
- They used nonprofit presence per capita and other behavioral indicators.

## MRI continuation lead
A Big Ten Academic Alliance catalog entry for Mediamark Research (MRI) Consumer Survey via SimplyAnalytics lists temporal coverage 2008–2019, restricted/licensed. This is a high-value lead for extending behavioral Putnam-like measures beyond 2004, but exact questionnaire continuity with the Hawes 22 items and Putnam P04/P09/P10/P11/P12/P14 must be audited before adoption.

## P13 trust lead
Published literature confirms GSS 'Most people can be trusted' is the canonical wording. State-level use exists but standard public GSS geography/sample is not a clean 50-state annual panel. A 2025 Pew large survey uses the identical wording and reports state estimates, but it is outside our 2000–2023 target. Do not substitute it into target period.

## P12/P14 provenance
Putnam's 'entertained at home' and 'Most people are honest' came from DDB Needham Life Style archive. Putnam's own research page confirms the DDB selected-question archive covers 1975–1999 and includes Entertain at home. No verified 2000–2023 50-state repeated public continuation has yet been established.

## Implication
Do not treat all 14 original Putnam components as annually observable 2000–2023. Use exact/near-direct observed waves only, keep missing years missing, and preserve S0 vs modern S_t separation. Final variable adoption remains the user's decision.
