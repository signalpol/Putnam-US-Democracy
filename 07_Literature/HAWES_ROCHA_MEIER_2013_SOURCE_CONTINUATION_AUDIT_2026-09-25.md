# Hawes–Rocha–Meier (2013) source-continuation audit — 2026-09-25

## Finding
The 2013 SPPQ article does NOT simply extend Putnam's original 14 items. It constructs a new dynamic behavioral social-capital index from 22 items, deliberately excluding informal sociability and generalized trust because annual state estimates were not feasible.

## Core raw sources
1. MediaMark Research (MRI), Survey of the American Consumer — >20,000 respondents, biannual, contiguous 48 states. Main behavioral items.
2. State/national election turnout (SAUS in paper).
3. National Center for Charitable Statistics (NCCS), nonprofit organizations per capita, 1995 onward.
4. Catalogue for Philanthropy Generosity Index, 1995 onward.
5. MRI public television contribution items.

## MRI behavioral items reported in paper
Organizational membership: fraternal order; religious club; civic club; veteran club; country club; body of local government.
Public affairs: voted; wrote elected official; wrote editor; contacted radio/TV; wrote something published; addressed public meeting; visited elected official; political party/candidate work; fundraising.
Volunteerism: contributed to public television; contribution amount composite; actively worked as nonpolitical volunteer; active part in local civic issue.
Plus turnout, NPO density, generosity index = 22-item index.

## Public replication data
Daniel Hawes publicly uploaded Public_SCData.dta (54.41 KB) and PUBLIC SC CODEBOOK in April 2014. ResearchGate also lists later HawesSPPQ ReplicationData / Codebook / ReplicationFile (2017).

## Post-2004 continuation
MRI's Survey of the American Consumer continued after 2004. SimplyAnalytics documentation/catalog proves MRI Consumer Survey annual vintages at least 2008–2019; MRI-Simmons states the Survey of the American Consumer continued until it was merged with Simmons National Consumer Study into MRI-Simmons USA in 2021.

However, the 2008–2019 MRI data are licensed/restricted. Exact continuity of each of Hawes's behavioral questions must be checked in the licensed variable catalog before treating them as a continuous series. Public web evidence proves dataset continuation, not yet exact 22-item continuity.

## Research implication
Best continuity strategy:
A. acquire the authors' 1986–2004 public replication dataset/codebook and use 2000–2004 overlap;
B. audit MRI Consumer Survey 2005+ / licensed 2008–2019 for exact Hawes items;
C. audit MRI-Simmons USA 2021–2023 for retained equivalents;
D. where exact MRI continuity fails, keep observed gaps and use separate CPS/CBP/IRS sources rather than silently splice unlike measures.

No interpolation; no adoption without user decision.
